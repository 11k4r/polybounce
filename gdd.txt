GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT: COSMIC KINETICS (Working Title)

GENRE: Physics-Puzzle / Idle Tycoon
PLATFORM: Mobile (iOS/Android) & PC
ENGINE: Unity 2D (Standard RigidBody2D)

1. CORE PREMISE & ELEVATOR PITCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cosmic Kinetics is a sensory, physics-based idle game where players manipulate 
real-time 2D physics inside geometric arenas to generate kinetic energy (cash). 
The game scales from manually dropping a single ball into a spinning circle to 
managing a multiverse of simulated physics systems. 

The core strategy relies on finding the mathematical "Sweet Spot"—tuning sliders 
for gravity, bounciness, friction, and rotation to maximize collisions without 
clogging the system. It combines the hypnotic visual satisfaction of a screensaver 
with the deep, strategic progression of a tycoon game.


2. THE GAMEPLAY LOOP (PLAYER JOURNEY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Phase 1: The Grind (0-5 mins)
  Player taps the screen to spawn a heavy ball. It hits a wall, earns $1, and 
  despawns. Player repeats this to buy their first "Auto-Spawner".

- Phase 2: The Puzzle (5-30 mins)
  Balls drop automatically. Player uses cash to unlock Physics Sliders 
  (Bounciness, Gravity, Rotation Speed). They actively tweak the physics to keep 
  the balls bouncing as long and fast as possible to maximize Bounces Per Second (BPS).

- Phase 3: The Arsenal (1-5 Hours)
  Player unlocks new physics toys: Bumpers, Portals, and unique ball classes 
  (Ghost Balls, Heavy Bombs, Sticky Slime).

- Phase 4: Scaling Up (Days)
  Player zooms out to the Cosmic Scale, buying entirely new shapes and systems 
  to run in the background.

- Phase 5: Prestige (Weeks)
  The player collapses their universe for Meta-Currency to permanently alter the 
  game's fundamental physics laws.


3. THE COSMIC SCALE (PROGRESSION TIERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent CPU lag, real-time physics ONLY run at the Micro View. Zooming out 
switches the game to lightweight mathematical simulation based on average BPS.

- TIER 1: The Micro View (The Laboratory)
  Visual: A single rotating geometric shape. 
  Mechanics: Real-time 2D physics. Up to 1,000 bouncing balls.
  Goal: Tune sliders to find the max BPS for this specific shape.

- TIER 2: The Mid View (The Factory)
  Visual: 3 to 5 Shapes connected by glowing energy lines.
  Mechanics: Physics suspended. Game calculates income using background math. 
  Goal: Create synergies (e.g., A Triangle shape feeds a multiplier into a Square).

- TIER 3: The Macro View (The Galaxy)
  Visual: A scrollable star map. Systems are glowing nodes.
  Mechanics: Tycoon management. Numbers scale into Trillions/Quadrillions.
  Goal: Buy new Systems with unique physical constraints (e.g., "The Aqua System" 
  has high drag and low gravity).


4. NESTED PRESTIGE SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent the game from feeling slow, players can "reset" at different scales:

- Micro-Prestige (Shape Overclock): Every 15-30 minutes.
  Action: Reset balls and sliders in ONE shape.
  Reward: Shape gets a neon visual upgrade and a permanent base cash multiplier.

- Meso-Prestige (System Supernova): Every few hours.
  Action: Reset all shapes in a specific System.
  Reward: Unlocks internal Arena Builders (bumpers, portals, gravity wells).

- Macro-Prestige (The Big Crunch): Every few weeks.
  Action: Destroy the entire Galaxy. Cash resets to $0.
  Reward: Grants "Cosmic Dust." Spent in a permanent Tech Tree for rule-breaking 
  modifiers (e.g., "Shapes spin 2x faster forever").


5. SCENES & UI ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The game uses a Single-Scene Architecture for gameplay to prevent load times from 
interrupting the physics engine or background music.

[SCENE 1: MAIN MENU]
- Title screen, hypnotic background shapes.
- Play Button, Sandbox Button, Cloud Login, Leaderboards, General Settings (Audio).

[SCENE 2: CAREER MODE] (Where 99% of gameplay happens)
- State A: The Micro UI (Zoomed In)
  * Top: Bank (Cash) and Current BPS.
  * Center: Active shape and bouncing balls.
  * Bottom Tab 1: Shop (Auto-spawners, Max Ball Limits).
  * Bottom Tab 2: Physics (Sliders for Gravity, Rotation, Mass, etc.).
  * Bottom Tab 3: Armory (Drag-and-drop bumpers and ball types).

- State B: The Mid/Macro UI (Zoomed Out)
  * Overlay Canvas with drag-to-pan and pinch-to-zoom controls.
  * System Upgrades and Galaxy Node purchasing buttons.

[UI PANELS] (Pop-ups over the Career Scene)
- Welcome Back: Offline earnings calculator. Includes "Watch Ad for 2X" button.
- Tech Tree: Sprawling skill tree for Cosmic Dust meta-upgrades.
- The Big Crunch Warning: Dramatic red UI, requires "Hold 3 Secs to Confirm".


6. GAME MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Career Mode: The main Tycoon progression loop.
- Sandbox Mode (The God-Mode Toy): 
  * Accessed from Main Menu. 
  * Money doesn't exist. All sliders, shapes, and ball types are unlocked.
  * Used by players for stress relief and to test "Sweet Spot" slider strategies 
    before building them in Career Mode. Used by Devs to test bug-free physics.


7. SOCIAL & COMPETITIVE (LEADERBOARDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No real-time networked physics (avoids desync/lag). Competition is asynchronous.

- Global Ladders: Ranks players by Total Wealth, Highest single-shape BPS, and 
  Fastest time to trigger a "Big Crunch".
- The Daily Anomaly: A daily challenge mode. Every player in the world is given 
  the exact same weird shape with corrupted physics (e.g., Star shape, zero gravity, 
  high friction) and 3 minutes on the clock. Highest score goes to the Daily Board.


8. AUDIO, VISUALS, & SENSORY "JUICE"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Generative Audio: Every wall-hit triggers a musical chime (pentatonic scale). 
  As BPS increases, the game naturally generates ambient, rhythmic electronic music.
- Visuals: Dark void background. Balls leave glowing neon trails. 
- Feedback: Subtle screen shakes on heavy ball impacts. Haptic phone vibrations 
  for UI clicks and Overclocks.


9. MONETIZATION & RETENTION (NO BETTING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Strictly adheres to Tycoon/Idle best practices. No gambling/betting mechanics.
- Rewarded Ads: "Watch 30s ad to double your Offline Earnings." (Highest revenue).
- Time Skips: Spend premium currency (Dark Matter) to instantly gain 4 hours of cash.
- Cosmetics: Purchase synthwave skins, custom hit-sounds, or rainbow neon trails.
- Remove Ads: Premium one-time purchase.
